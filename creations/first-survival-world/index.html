About this world

Melon Survival is the very first survival world I ever created. I don't remember the exact year it began, but it marks the start of my Minecraft journey and many of the building ideas that would eventually shape my future worlds.

The world earned its name naturally. My first home was built in a jungle, and as a monkey, it felt like the perfect place to settle. For a long time, melons were my main source of food, so "Melon Survival" simply became the name of the world.

The heart of the world is my tree house, which still stands today. It was my very first base, my oldest surviving build, and the place where I spent most of my early adventures. Looking back, it's still my favorite part of the entire world because it reminds me of how I first experienced Minecraft.

Unlike my current survival world, this one eventually included the use of cheats. I started using in-game commands to give myself items and make building easier. Over time, that took away much of the challenge, and I eventually lost interest in continuing the world.

Rather than trying to fix it, I decided to start over. That decision led directly to my current survival world, where I made one simple rule: no cheats would ever be used. In many ways, Melon Survival became the lesson that inspired everything I've built since.

Today, this world remains exactly as it was left—a snapshot of where my Minecraft journey began. If there's one thing I hope visitors notice, it's just how much the world reflects its age, especially the original tree house that started it all. While this world has been officially retired, it will always be an important part of my history as a Minecraft player.
