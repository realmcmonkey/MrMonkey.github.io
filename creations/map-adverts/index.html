Minecraft Bedrock Add-on / In Development

Mr. Monkey Map Adverts is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on made to give map creators more ways to add advertising and extra detail throughout their worlds

When building a city, store, attraction, entertainment area or another detailed Minecraft map, sometimes the little things are what make an area actually feel complete. Real places are filled with signs, advertisements and displays, but recreating those details in Minecraft can be difficult when you're limited to normal blocks, paintings and signs.

That's where Mr. Monkey Map Adverts comes in.

The add-on is designed around giving builders dedicated adverts that can be placed throughout a Minecraft world and incorporated directly into a build instead of having to construct every advertisement manually out of blocks.

Made for map building

Mr. Monkey Map Adverts isn't meant to replace the things you're building. The adverts are there to add another layer of detail to them.

You could have adverts throughout a large city, inside commercial areas, around entertainment venues, alongside pathways or anywhere else where a normal Minecraft build feels like it needs something more.

Different areas of a map can use adverts differently, which means they don't all have to feel like copies of the same thing.

More detail without rebuilding everything

One of the ideas behind Map Adverts is being able to add smaller details to an existing build without needing to redesign the entire area around them.

Place an advert where it makes sense, configure it using the features available in the add-on, and let it become part of the environment.

This makes them useful both while creating a brand-new area and when returning to an older part of a world that could use some extra detail.

Designed to actually belong in the world.

The goal isn't just to put pictures into Minecraft.

The adverts are intended to look like actual pieces of the environment. When they're used in the right places, they should feel like something that was always supposed to be there.

That means you can use them as smaller details in the background or make an advert a much more noticeable part of an area depending on what you're building.

Built for Minecraft Bedrock

Mr. Monkey Map Adverts is being developed specifically as a Minecraft Bedrock add-on.

This is also a project that I plan to continue improving. The current version represents where the add-on is today, while future versions can continue expanding and improving what map creators are able to do with it.
